Questions customers usually ask

Does it come in glass?

Yes. Still and sparkling mineral water options are available in reusable glass bottles, depending on the brand selected.

How fast is delivery?

Orders are generally accepted throughout the day, with same-day scheduling where possible and a message shortly before arrival.

Frequently asked questions

How do deposits work?

Each reusable crate, jug, or bottle set carries a small deposit. The deposit comes back when the empties are returned in acceptable condition.

Is delivery really free?

Glass-bottle delivery is positioned as free, and returns are also collected. Jug orders may need a minimum quantity for free delivery.

Can customers return water they do not like?

Returns can be discussed if the packaging is intact and the product is still in good condition. Container counts and transport distance may affect the final resolution.

How should customers get their deposit back quickly?

Tell the business early when empties need to be collected, especially if a pickup must happen before a move-out, handover, or deadline.
